Scaling direction: log(parameters) vs. task performance

Each point is one open-weight model. Lines are per-lab OLS fits of task score on log(params) (unconditional on instruct/date, for display only — the paper's Aₓ controls for both). qwen and allenai show clear positive scaling slopes across tasks; google shows a much flatter, noisier response over the same task set — consistent with google being one of the 3 labs (of 14) without a statistically measurable scaling response in the fitted model.
